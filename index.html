Screen 1 of 3 • Navigation & Progression

The World Map Screen

A vertically scrolling, illustrated botanical journey. Players advance by conquering numbered stepping-stone medallions that weave through blooming courtyard gardens, stone arches, and sculpted topiaries.

🪵 Carved Level Medallions
Level nodes use 3 distinct visual states: completed wooden plaques with 1-3 earned gold stars; an elevated active node pulsating with a radiant golden aura; and chained river stones with brass padlocks for locked stages.
📜 Storybook Parchment HUD
Restrained top timber frame displays current courtyard area ("Courtyard Haven"), coin balance (250) and star balance (4) without visual clutter.
🌿 Tactile "Play Next" Button
Capsule-shaped primary action button in the bottom dock with a 4px 3D bevel rim, foliage embellishments, and snappy tactile touch feedback.
📖 Garden Journal Tab
Bottom left notebook affordance with task notifications, allowing seamless toggling between puzzle progression and 3D garden restoration.
Screen 2 of 3 • Core Gameplay Board

Match-3 Gameplay Screen

A crystal-clear, readable 8x8 match-3 board framed in rich carved cedar timber. Engineered with strict dual-coding: each piece is immediately recognizable by color and unique silhouette, ensuring rapid accessibility and zero fatigue.

🎯 Restrained Objective HUD
Hanging parchment goal tags showing real-time counters (e.g. 15 Emerald Leaves, 10 Wild Berries) with tactile bump-animations upon decrement.
⏳ Center Moves Counter
Large, prominent circular brass counter reading moves remaining (22 Moves) with warm golden numerals, preventing accidental loss.
✨ Blossom Bomb Special Piece
Spawned by 4-in-a-row matches: a radiant multi-hue flower bud emitting a sparkling particle aura that clears a 3x3 radius upon activation.
🛠️ Garden Tool Boosters Drawer
Bottom wooden tray holding garden trowels (single tile clear) and watering cans (row clear) with remaining inventory badges.
Screen 3 of 3 • Metagame Transformation

Garden Courtyard Restoration

The emotional reward of Project Bloomtrail. Players spend stars earned from puzzle victories to transform the overgrown courtyard fountain from cracked, vine-tangled decay into a vibrant, flowing limestone monument.

⛲ Visible Before-and-After
A tangible transformation: dry cracked basins and choking ivy dissolve into crystal-clear flowing fountain waters and blooming flowerbeds.
⭐ Star Progression Economy
Every star won in puzzles has immediate, visible value. No fake timers, no paywalls, no simulator grinding.
🔨 Task Card Action Control
Carved timber drawer shows active task ("Restore Fountain Basin"), thumbnail preview, 1 Star cost badge, and green "RESTORE" button with brass hammer.
☀️ Golden Hour Atmosphere
Warm sunlit morning directional lighting, floating floral petals, and soft ambient bird ambience create a cozy, relaxing retreat.
